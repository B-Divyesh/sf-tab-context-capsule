The three-stop line

From browser noise to a clean handoff

No summary bot. No mystery cloud. Just the small piece of human context tab managers leave behind.

    Choose the carriages

    Select the useful tabs from your current window. Pinned and private tabs stay out by default.

    Write the meaning

    Name the project, add one line per page, arrange the order, and record the next move.

    Seal and depart

    Close the originals only when you choose. Reopen later, or hand off a readable Markdown or JSON ticket.
